# AI-Native Insurance Platform | Federato


The first AI‑native insurance platform designed for the full policy lifecycle. Drive smarter risk decisions, faster quoting, and portfolio alignment with Federato.

## Underwriting Solutions
- [Underwriting Platform](https://www.federato.ai/platform/underwriting-platform): Never drift off underwriting strategy in execution, ensuring alignment with business goals.
